working title:
    Connect Four Online

description:
    The project will provide a platform (hosted on web) to play connect four online
    with other people. It will utilize client-server communications to allow users
    to play against people, either through matchmaking or by entering a unique
    identifier which allows users to play against another specific user (lobbies?)

target user:
    Intermediate to Advanced connect four players who want to improve their strategy
    by playing against different algorithms (minimax, negamax), plus some
    optimizations. How long can you last against the bot?

programming language:
    most likely rust (since same lang for help proj)

    iced for frontend (cross platform incl. web)
    tokio for networking (tcp client-server)
    serde + serde_json for serializing + deserializing
    sqlite for database 

    must build in this order:
        - online stuff
        - then ai
